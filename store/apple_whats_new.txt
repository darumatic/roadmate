Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version — trip timer fixes:

• The trip timer now counts from the moment you press Start and keeps ticking every second, even before the first GPS fix arrives. It could previously sit on "0m 0s" for a whole trip if you started somewhere with no signal
• Saved trips record the real elapsed time of the run, including the wait for GPS at the start, so the log matches what you watched on screen
• The GPS status under the speedometer now says what is really happening: amber while it waits for a fix, red if the signal drops. It used to show green as soon as tracking started, even when no fix had arrived

Live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED site status, voted in real time by other truckies
• Site alerts as you approach an inspection site, on screen or in the background
• Blitz alerts when unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the nearest sites with GPS
• Favourites for the sites on your regular runs
• Trip log with audible speed alerts
• Camera Times: point-to-point average-speed camera corridors, with a live "Time me" timer for your run
• Add missing sites
• Share RoadMate with another driver straight from the Info tab
• Sign in with Google or Apple
