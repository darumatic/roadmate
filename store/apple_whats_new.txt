Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version:

• Smarter approach alerts: where a northbound and a southbound station share the one spot, RoadMate now uses your direction of travel and asks only about the station on your side of the road. Once yours is answered and you're within 100 m, one quick follow-up asks about the other side
• Open any site in your maps app straight from its card, for turn-by-turn directions to the gate
• Road names: pick a unique handle like "Dusty Roadtrain" and it signs your votes, your activity reports and any site you add, so other drivers can see who called it. Roll the dice for a suggestion or type your own — you're asked once, and you can change it any time from the User tab
• Participation points, levels and badges: every vote and report earns points and moves you up the levels, with your level shown beside your name on the reports you post. The new Achievements page collects the badges you've earned — including Trailblazer, for adding a site to the map
• Reporting stays open to every driver, no sign-in needed, from within 3 km of a site
• Small behind-the-scenes fixes

Live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED site status, voted in real time by other truckies
• Site alerts as you approach an inspection site, on screen or in the background
• Blitz alerts when unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the nearest sites with GPS
• Directions to any site in your maps app
• Favourites for the sites on your regular runs
• Trip log with audible speed alerts
• Camera Times: point-to-point average-speed camera corridors, with a live "Time me" timer for your run
• Add missing sites
• Points, levels and badges for the drivers who keep the map honest
• Share RoadMate with another driver straight from the Info tab
• Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favourites, trips and reports together — optional, browsing and posting work without an account

Safe travels!
