Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version — trustworthy reports, better site alerts, and notices from the team:

• Reports and status votes now need a free sign-in with Apple or Google, so every report comes from a real account
• Tap OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED or Report activity while signed out and RoadMate asks once, then posts what you were sending — nothing to retype
• Signing in keeps everything you already have: your favourites, your trips and your past reports all carry over
• Browsing, Nearby, favourites and adding a site stay open to everyone, no account needed
• Important notices from the RoadMate team now appear as a banner at the top of the app
• Site alerts now wait for you: the alert stays on screen until you answer or dismiss it, instead of vanishing after a few seconds
• It counts the distance down as you close in, and clears itself once the site is behind you
• Dismissed one? RoadMate asks again inside 100 m, when the site is in sight
• Alerts still work in the background, phone in your pocket, and only fire when you are driving toward a site
• Turn alerts on or off with the arrow icon on Home
• Permission requests are now asked one at a time, so the notification and location prompts no longer land on top of each other
• Site moderators can now suspend an account that floods the app with junk reports — for a day, or for good
• A suspended account keeps full read access — live site status, alerts, speedo and trips — it just can't post
• Site moderators are no longer held back by the action limit, so bad reports and stale statuses get cleaned up faster
• Small behind-the-scenes fixes

Live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED site status, voted in real time by other truckies
• Site alerts as you approach an inspection site, on screen or in the background
• Blitz alerts when unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the nearest sites with GPS
• Favourites for the sites on your regular runs

• Add missing sites
• Share RoadMate with another driver straight from the Info tab
• Sign in with Apple or Google

Safe travels!
