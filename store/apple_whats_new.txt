Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version — site alerts:

• RoadMate now warns you as you approach an inspection site and asks for its status — one tap for OPEN, BLITZ or CLOSED
• Alerts keep working while the app is in the background, so you still get the heads-up with maps on screen or the phone in your pocket
• Only fires when you're actually driving toward a site, and at most once per site — no nagging when you're parked or heading away
• Turn it on or off any time with the arrow icon on the Home screen
• Adding a site now captures its GPS position with one tap, so it appears in Nearby straight away
