Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version — reporting is open to every driver again:

• No sign-in needed any more to tap OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED or Report activity — posting is open to everyone again
• To keep reports honest they now come from the road: posting works when you're within 3 km of a site, the same range where RoadMate starts asking "what's the status?"
• RoadMate asks for your location as you post; if location is off or the site is too far away, a message tells you exactly why
• Signing in stays optional — it still keeps your favourites, your trips and your past reports together under your account
• Browsing, Nearby, favourites and adding a site are unchanged and open to everyone
• Small behind-the-scenes fixes

Live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED site status, voted in real time by other truckies
• Site alerts as you approach an inspection site, on screen or in the background
• Blitz alerts when unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the nearest sites with GPS
• Favourites for the sites on your regular runs
• Trip log with audible speed alerts
• Camera Times: point-to-point average-speed camera corridors, with a live "Time me" timer for your run
• Add missing sites
• Share RoadMate with another driver straight from the Info tab
• Sign in with Apple or Google

Safe travels!
