Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

New in this version:

• New status: Camera Only / BGD (boom gate down). One big blue button under Open/Working, Blitz and Closed — on every site card and on the alert you get as you approach a site
• Pressing it sets the site's status, like the other three — it no longer adds a line under Recent reports with every press
• Your vote or report now shows on the site card the moment you tap — no waiting on a weak signal, and no reason to tap twice
• Fixed: change your mind within seconds — say Camera Only, then Closed — and it's your correction everyone sees, not the first tap
• Fixed: a site's status now stays current only while someone has actually reported a status. Reporting a queue, delays or police still shows up on the site, but no longer brings an old Open, Blitz or Closed back as if it were new
• Small behind-the-scenes fixes

Live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live OPEN / BLITZ / CLOSED / CAMERA ONLY site status, voted in real time by other truckies
• Site alerts as you approach an inspection site, on screen or in the background
• Blitz alerts when unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the nearest sites with GPS
• Directions to any site in your maps app
• Favourites for the sites on your regular runs
• Trip log with audible speed alerts
• Camera Times: point-to-point average-speed camera corridors, with a live "Time me" timer for your run
• Add missing sites
• Points, levels and badges for the drivers who keep the map honest
• Share RoadMate with another driver straight from the Info tab
• Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favourites, trips and reports together — optional, browsing and posting work without an account

Safe travels!
