RoadMate AU

Built by Truckies, for Truckies.

Turn your phone into the ultimate trucking companion. Whether you’re doing local runs or crossing the country, RoadMate AU puts the tools you need in one easy-to-use app—helping you stay informed, monitor your trip, and support the Australian trucking community.

🚛 GPS Speedometer & Trip Tools

RoadMate AU features a powerful GPS Speedometer designed specifically for professional drivers, including:

* Real-time GPS speed display
* Average speed tracking
* Speed limit checker (where available)
* Custom maximum speed warning
* Trip logger to record your distance, driving time, average speed, and journey history

Everything you need to keep track of your trip in one place.

📍 NHVR Inspection Sites

Stay informed with Australia’s growing community-powered NHVR inspection site database.

* View NHVR inspection sites across Australia
* Check community-reported Open, Blitz, and Closed site activity
* Find nearby inspection sites
* Search sites by state or location
* Save your favourite sites for quick access

🤝 Built by the Trucking Community

RoadMate AU is powered by drivers who help each other.

* Submit live reports to keep site activity up to date
* Add new NHVR inspection sites to expand the database
* Help thousands of fellow truckies stay informed on the road

Every contribution helps make the app better for everyone.

⭐ Why Truck Drivers Love RoadMate AU

* Fast and easy to use
* Designed specifically for Australian heavy vehicle drivers
* Community-powered information
* Modern, reliable, and regularly updated
* Everything in one app—no switching between multiple apps
