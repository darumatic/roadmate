Welcome to RoadMate AU — know before you roll.

RoadMate shows the live status of NHVR heavy-vehicle inspection sites across
Australia, powered by drivers on the road:

• Live site status — see whether a site is OPEN, running a BLITZ or CLOSED, voted in real time by other truckies
• Blitz alerts — a banner warns you the moment unusual enforcement activity is reported
• Browse by state or find the sites nearest to you with GPS
• Favourites — star the sites on your regular runs for one-tap access
• Trip log with speed alerts — record your trips and get an audible warning when you drift over the limit
• Add missing sites and help the community stay informed
• Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favourites across devices

Safe travels, and know before you roll.
