Delete your RoadMate account

To delete your RoadMate AU account and associated data, email info@roadmate.club with the subject "Account deletion request", sent from the Google account email you use to sign in.

We will delete your account and the personal data associated with it — your authentication record and account details (email, display name) — within 30 days. Site status reports you submitted may be retained as part of the anonymous community history, no longer linked to you.

If you used RoadMate anonymously (without signing in), no personal account data is held against your name; uninstalling the app removes the anonymous identifier from your device.

Questions? See our Privacy Policy or contact info@roadmate.club.
