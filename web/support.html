Support

We usually reply within a couple of business days.

Need a hand with RoadMate AU? The quickest way to reach us is by email — see the contact card below. In the meantime, these answers cover the most common questions.

Frequently asked questions

  • What do OPEN, BLITZ and CLOSED mean? They are live, community-voted statuses for each NHVR inspection site. OPEN means the site is operating, BLITZ means drivers are reporting unusually heavy activity, and CLOSED means the site is not operating. Votes age out automatically, so the status reflects recent reports.
  • Nearby shows nothing or the wrong sites. Nearby needs location permission. Check your device settings, grant location access to RoadMate, and try again — the rest of the app works without it.
  • I added a site but can't see it. New sites are reviewed before they appear publicly, to keep the data trustworthy. They usually show up within a few days.
  • Where are my trips stored? Saved trips live only on your device. Sign in with Apple or Google to keep your favourites across devices.
  • How do I delete my account? In the app, open the User tab, sign in if you aren't already, and tap "Delete account" — or see roadmate.club/delete-account.html.
  • Something looks wrong or broken. Email us with your device model, app version (shown at the bottom of the Info tab) and what happened, and we'll look into it.
